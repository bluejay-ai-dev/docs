## Connect Slack

<Card title="Connect Slack" icon="/logo/slack.svg" href="https://app.getbluejay.ai/settings/integrations?tab=slack&connect=slack">
  Connects Slack to the workspace you currently have open. Approve Bluejay in Slack to finish.
</Card>

## Commands

Two commands work today. More are on the way.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/bluejay link` | If your Slack account is already linked, Bluejay names the Bluejay account. If it is not, Bluejay sends a private **Connect your accounts** button. Sign in on that page to finish the link. |
| `/bluejay unlink` | Removes the link. Bluejay will not act for you in Slack until you connect again. |

Any other `/bluejay` command points you back to these two.

## Link your account

Bluejay answers only for a Slack user whose account is linked to a member of the connected workspace.

The first time you message the bot, Bluejay checks your Slack email. When that email matches one member of the workspace, the link is saved and the bot answers.

The email match runs only before the first link. After `/bluejay unlink`, run `/bluejay link` and use **Connect your accounts**.
