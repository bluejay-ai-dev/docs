Bluejay AI is an assistant inside the Bluejay app. It reads the page you are on and answers questions about your simulation runs and your production calls, so you can find out why calls fail without reading every transcript yourself. It can also act on what it finds, for example by adding a test for a failure or starting Self-Improve, as described in [Act on the answer](#act-on-the-answer).

## Open Bluejay AI

To open Bluejay AI, click the round Bluejay logo button at the bottom right of the app. You can also press `Cmd+J` (Mac) or `Ctrl+J` (Windows). The **Bluejay AI** item in the sidebar opens the full Bluejay AI page.

Because Bluejay AI reads the page you are on, first open the simulation run or the Logs page you want to ask about. Your questions can then say "this run" or "these calls".

## Find what to improve

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Test">
    After a simulation run, ask:

    - "Why did the failed calls in this run fail?"
    - "Which Digital Humans failed, and what do they have in common?"
    - "What should I fix first on my agent?"
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Monitor">
    For production conversations, ask:

    - "Why did my calls fail this week?"
    - "What percent of inbound calls failed today, and which agent is worst?"
    - "Show me example calls where the caller asked for a human."
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Act on the answer

- **Edit the prompt.** Ask Bluejay AI to rewrite your agent's prompt to fix one failure pattern, then update your agent with the new prompt.
- **Start Self-Improve.** Ask Bluejay AI to improve the agent against a simulation. Bluejay AI shows a preview, and the run starts when you confirm it. See [Self-Improve](/improve/self-improve).
- **Add a test.** Ask Bluejay AI to create Digital Humans for the failure, linked to your simulation. See [Digital Humans](/test/simulations/digital-humans).
- **Measure it.** Ask Bluejay AI to create a custom metric that grades the failure on every call. See [custom metrics](/test/custom-metrics/overview).
- **Chart it.** Ask Bluejay AI to build a dashboard, for example "Make a dashboard of pass rate by agent for the last month". It can also add, change, and remove widgets. See [Dashboards](/monitor/dashboards).
- **Import a workflow.** Attach a document to a Bluejay AI chat and ask for a workflow. After you approve the draft, Bluejay AI saves it to the agent. You can import only to an agent that has no workflow provider.
