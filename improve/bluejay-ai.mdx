Bluejay AI is the assistant inside the Bluejay app. Ask it about your runs and calls, and it can also make the change for you.

<video controls muted playsInline preload="metadata" className="w-full rounded-xl" src="/videos/bluejay-ai-demo.mp4"></video>

## What you can do

- **Find why calls fail.** It reads the run or calls you have open and tells you why they failed.
- **Create and run a simulation.** Describe the callers you want, and it makes the Digital Humans and offers to start the run. See [Create a Simulation](/test/simulations/create#let-bluejay-ai-do-it).
- **Add a test for a failure.** It creates Digital Humans that repeat the failure. See [Digital Human Reference](/test/simulations/digital-humans).
- **Measure a failure.** It creates a [custom metric](/test/custom-metrics/overview) that grades the failure on every call.
- **Fix the prompt.** It rewrites your agent's prompt for one failure pattern, and you update your agent with it.
- **Start Self-Improve.** It shows a preview and starts the run when you confirm. See [Self-Improve](/improve/self-improve).
- **Build a dashboard.** It creates a dashboard or changes its widgets. See [Dashboards](/monitor/dashboards).
- **Import a workflow.** Attach a document, and it drafts the agent's workflow for you to approve. This works only for an agent that has no workflow provider.

## Open Bluejay AI

Click the round Bluejay logo at the bottom right of the app, or press `Cmd+J` (Mac) or `Ctrl+J` (Windows). Open the run or the Logs page first, because Bluejay AI reads the page you are on. Your question can then say "this run" or "these calls".

The **Bluejay AI** item in the sidebar opens a full-page chat.
