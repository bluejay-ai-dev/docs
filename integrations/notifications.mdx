Bluejay sends a notification to each destination you turn on for an alert or an uptime monitor.

## Connect

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Slack">
1. Open **Settings**, then **Integrations**, then the **Slack** tab.
2. Click **Connect**.
3. In Slack, review the permissions and click **Allow**.

![Connect to Slack](/images/integrations-slack-connect.png)

To remove the connection, click **Disconnect** on the same tab.
</Tab>
<Tab title="PagerDuty">
1. In PagerDuty, open the service that receives Bluejay incidents.
2. Under **Integrations**, add an **Events API V2** integration.
3. Copy the **Integration Key**.
4. In Bluejay, open **Settings**, then **Integrations**, then the **PagerDuty** tab.
5. Paste the key into **Routing Key** and click **Save Changes**.
</Tab>
<Tab title="Better Stack">
1. In Better Stack, open **API tokens**, then **Team-based tokens**.
2. Select the team whose on-call rotation receives Bluejay incidents.
3. Under **Uptime API tokens**, create a token and copy it.
4. In Bluejay, open **Settings**, then **Integrations**, then the **Better Stack** tab.
5. Paste the token into **API token** and click **Save Changes**.

Bluejay stores the token encrypted. Better Stack needs no other setup, and the free plan works.
</Tab>
<Tab title="Email">
Email needs no connection. Bluejay sends to your team members.
</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Turn on notifications

1. Open an alert in **Alerts**, or a monitor in **Uptime**.
2. Turn on **Slack Notifications**, **PagerDuty Notifications**, **Better Stack Notifications**, or **Email Notifications**.
3. For Slack, select a channel.
4. For email, select **All Team Members** or **Specific People**.
5. Save.

A toggle stays off until you connect its destination. On an alert, click **Send test notification** to check the setup.

![Slack Notifications in the alert dialog](/images/integrations-slack-alarm-dialog.png)

## Incidents

PagerDuty and Better Stack receive incidents. Your escalation policy in that tool decides who gets the page.

| Source | Opens an incident | Resolves the incident |
|---|---|---|
| Uptime monitor | When the monitor goes DOWN | When the monitor recovers |
| Observability alert | When the alert triggers | PagerDuty: when the alert recovers |
| Simulation alert | One incident per run | Never. Resolve it in PagerDuty or Better Stack. |

Each uptime monitor uses one fixed dedup key. A DOWN and its recovery update the same incident, and a second DOWN does not open a new incident while the first one is open.
