Bluejay tests and monitors voice, chat, and email agents. Pick the channel your customers use, then open its page to connect the agent.

| Channel | What Bluejay tests | Where to connect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voice | Calls with your agent | [Phone Number](/integrations/phone-number) |
| Chat | Text conversations with your agent | [Chat Completions](/integrations/openai-compatible) |
| Email | Emails to your agent and its replies | [Email](/integrations/email) |

## Voice

A Voice agent talks with customers on a call. Digital Humans call your agent, or your agent calls them, and Bluejay records, transcribes, and grades each call.

Connect it with a [phone number](/integrations/phone-number), [SIP](/integrations/sip), [WebSocket](/integrations/websockets), [LiveKit](/integrations/livekit), [Pipecat](/integrations/pipecat), or [OpenAI Realtime](/integrations/openai-realtime). If you build on a voice platform, connect it with [Vapi](/integrations/vapi), [Retell](/integrations/retell), [ElevenLabs](/integrations/eleven-labs), [Bland](/integrations/bland), or [Sierra](/integrations/sierra).

## Chat

A Text agent talks with customers in text. This includes SMS: create a Text agent with the **SMS** connection type, as described in [Phone Number](/integrations/phone-number).

For other chat agents, use [Chat Completions](/integrations/openai-compatible) if your agent answers OpenAI-style requests. Otherwise use an [HTTP Webhook](/integrations/http-webhooks). To test a chatbot platform, connect [Kore.ai](/integrations/kore).

[Amelia](/integrations/amelia), [Google CES](/integrations/google-ces), and [Dialogflow CX](/integrations/google-dfcx) test by chat or by voice, depending on the agent's mode.

## Email

An Email agent answers messages sent to its inbox. Digital Humans email your agent, and Bluejay grades whether the replies pass authentication and arrive. Your inbox must [accept mail from Bluejay](/integrations/email#allow-bluejays-mail) first.

[Red Teaming](/test/red-teaming/overview) is not available for email agents yet.
