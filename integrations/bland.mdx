This guide walks you through connecting your Bland phone number's inbound calls to Bluejay's observability software. Bland agents connect through their phone number: Bluejay matches each webhook event to the agent whose number it belongs to.

<video controls className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" src="/videos/bland-walkthrough.mp4"></video>


### Quick Prerequisites

- **Bland Phone Number**
- **Bland Webhook Secret**
- **A Bluejay Account**

### Setting Up the Integration

### 1. Configure Organization Settings

1. Log into your **Bluejay Dashboard**
2. Click **Settings** in the bottom left corner
3. Open the **Integrations** tab and select **Bland**


![Navigate to Integrations](/images/integrations.png)

### 2. Adding Your Bland Key

1. Generate your Bland webhook signing secret in Bland, under the "General" tab of your organization's settings
2. Paste it into the **Webhook Secret** field
3. Click **Save Changes**

![Save API Key](/images/add-bland-secret.png)

### 3. Configuring Your Agent in Bland

1. **Copy the Webhook URL given** in the Webhook configuration dropdown 
2. Navigate to your Bland Phone Numbers configuration screen and scroll down to the **Advanced** dropdown
3. **Paste the Webhook URL into the Webhook URL field**
4. Click **Save Inbound Call Changes**


<video controls className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" src="/videos/add-link-bland.mov"></video>

### 4. Configuring Your Agent in Bluejay

1. Open your agent's settings and go to the **Connection Settings** tab
2. Choose the **Phone** tile under **Voice connection type**
3. Paste your Bland phone number into the **Phone Number** field, in full international format (e.g. `+15551234567`)
4. Click **Save Changes**
