A simulation tests your agent before real customers reach it. Bluejay sends [Digital Humans](/test/simulations/digital-humans), which are realistic test customers, to call or message your agent, then scores each conversation with [custom metrics](/metrics). This recipe takes you from a new account to your first set of results.

## Before you start

- A Bluejay account. [Sign up at app.getbluejay.ai](https://app.getbluejay.ai/auth/sign-up).
- The agent you want to test, reachable the same way your customers reach it, for example a phone number.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your agent">
    Click **+** in the sidebar header. Pick the mode and connection type that match how your customers reach the agent, so the test conversations take the same path as real ones. Choose **Inbound** or **Outbound** and paste the agent's system prompt, because Bluejay uses it to generate Digital Humans and metrics. See [Connect your agent](/test/simulations/create#connect-your-agent).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a simulation">
    Open **Simulations** in the sidebar and click **Create**. Choose your agent under **Agent** and pick the **Generate from Natural Language** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the customers you want">
    Write who calls and what they want, for example "10 frustrated customers who want to cancel their subscription". Bluejay turns the description into Digital Humans. See [Natural Language](/test/simulation-types/natural-language).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Digital Humans and run">
    Click **Generate Digital Humans** and read the list before you start, because each Digital Human places one conversation. Then click **Run**. Bluejay starts the conversations and opens the run page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the results">
    The run page shows the pass rate and one row for each conversation. Open the failed rows first: each one shows which metric failed and where in the conversation it went wrong. If a row did not finish as **Completed**, its [error code](/test/simulations/create#error-codes) says why.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix the agent and run again">
    Change your agent's prompt or flow, then open the simulation and click **Create New Run** to test the same Digital Humans again. Compare the pass rate with the first run.
  </Step>
</Steps>
