When your agent hands a caller to a person, a failed transfer leaves the caller on hold or cut off, and nobody on your team hears about it. This recipe grades every production call for whether its transfer went through, and notifies you the first time one fails.

## Before you start

- Production calls reach Bluejay from your voice platform or through the Evaluate API. See [Logs](/monitor/logs#get-conversations-into-logs).
- A notification channel is connected. See [Alerts](/monitor/alerts#connect-a-channel).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a transfer metric">
    In **Metrics**, click **Create**, then **Create manually**. Choose **LLM as a Judge** as the **Type** and **Pass/Fail** as the **Response type**, and write a prompt that passes when the caller was connected to the person they were transferred to and fails when the transfer dropped, timed out, or the agent came back saying it could not connect. See the [Prompting Guide](/test/custom-metrics/overview#prompting-guide) for how to word it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run it on every production call">
    In the same dialog, select your agent under **Agents** and set **Runs on** to **Observability**, so the metric grades your live calls. Turn on **Allow N/A Responses**, so calls without a transfer show Not Applicable instead of a pass.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an alert on the metric">
    In **Observability → Alerts**, click **Create Alert** and pick the metric under **Trigger**. Set it to fire when the metric **Equals** **False** for at least 1 event in the interval you choose, because one failed transfer is already a caller you lost. Under **Scope**, select your agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who gets notified">
    Under **Notify**, turn on the channel your team watches, then click **Send test notification** to check that it arrives. See [Alerts](/monitor/alerts#choose-where-each-alert-goes) for which channel fits which problem.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check graded calls in Logs">
    Open **Observability → Logs**. Each new call shows the metric's result, and you can read the transcript of a failed transfer to see where it broke.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The **Transfer Landed** metric type passes only when the transfer reaches one of your Digital Humans, so it fits [simulations](/test/simulations/create). Production transfers reach real people, so this recipe uses an LLM as a Judge metric instead.
</Note>

## Check the result

Calls that arrive after you save the metric show a Pass, Fail, or Not Applicable result in Logs. Calls that arrived earlier are not graded until you select them and click **Reevaluate**. If calls show **Not Evaluated**, your organization scores only a sample of production calls. If the metric fails calls that transferred correctly, add those calls to a [Metrics Lab](/test/custom-metrics/overview#metrics-lab) and adjust the prompt.
