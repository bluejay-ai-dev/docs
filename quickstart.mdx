A simulation sends Digital Humans to call your agent, then scores each Conversation with Custom Metrics.

You need a Bluejay account at [app.getbluejay.ai](https://app.getbluejay.ai). If you do not have one, [book a demo](https://cal.com/rohanv/15min).

## Connect your agent

Select your stack. Bluejay keeps your choice on other pages that use the same tabs.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Vapi">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Connect Your Agent dialog">
        In the sidebar header, click **+** (Add agent). Enter a **Name**, then set **Mode** to **Voice**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add your Vapi key">
        Under **Voice Connection Type**, select **VAPI**. Paste your Vapi private key into **Private Key**, then click **Save & Sync**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select the assistant">
        In **VAPI Assistant ID**, paste the ID or search by name. Click **Create**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
    Full setup: [Vapi](/integrations/vapi).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Retell">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Connect Your Agent dialog">
        In the sidebar header, click **+** (Add agent). Enter a **Name**, then set **Mode** to **Voice**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add your Retell key">
        Under **Voice Connection Type**, select **Retell**. Paste your Retell key into **API Key**, then click **Save & Sync**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select the agent">
        In **Retell Agent ID**, paste the ID or search by name. Click **Create**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
    Full setup: [Retell](/integrations/retell).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="LiveKit">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Connect Your Agent dialog">
        In the sidebar header, click **+** (Add agent). Enter a **Name**, then set **Mode** to **Voice**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add your LiveKit credentials">
        Under **Voice Connection Type**, select **LiveKit**. Enter **API Key**, **API Secret**, and **LiveKit URL**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Name the agent">
        Enter the **LiveKit Agent Name**. Click **Create**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
    Optional fields: **LiveKit Customer Tool Topic** and **LiveKit Metadata** (JSON). Full setup: [LiveKit](/integrations/livekit).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="ElevenLabs">

      </Step>
      <Step title="Add your ElevenLabs key">
        Under **Voice Connection Type**, select **ElevenLabs**. Paste your ElevenLabs key into **API Key**, then click **Save & Sync**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select the agent">
        In **ElevenLabs Agent ID**, paste the ID or search by name. Click **Create**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
    Full setup: [ElevenLabs](/integrations/eleven-labs).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Bland">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Connect Your Agent dialog">

      </Step>
      <Step title="Add your Bland key">
        Under **Voice Connection Type**, select **Bland**. Paste your Bland key into **API Key**, then click **Save & Sync**.

      <Step title="Select the pathway">
        Enter the **Bland Pathway ID**. If you use a web agent, enter the **Bland Web Agent ID**. Click **Create**.
      </Step>

    Full setup: [Bland](/integrations/bland).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Phone/SIP">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Connect Your Agent dialog">
        In the sidebar header, click **+** (Add agent). Enter a **Name**, then set **Mode** to **Voice**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the address">
        Under **Voice Connection Type**, select **Phone** or **SIP**. For **Phone**, enter the **Phone Number**. For **SIP**, enter the **SIP URI**, for example `sip:username@domain.com`.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Create the agent">
        Click **Create**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
    Full setup: [SIP](/simulation-integrations/sip).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="WebSocket">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Connect Your Agent dialog">
        In the sidebar header, click **+** (Add agent). Enter a **Name**, then set **Mode** to **Voice**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the endpoint">
        Under **Voice Connection Type**, select **Websocket**. Enter the **Websocket URL**, for example `wss://your-websocket-endpoint.com`.

  </Tab>

<Note>
  You can also save provider keys in **Settings** > **Integrations**. Use the **Sync Agents from** button for your provider to import all agents.
</Note>

## Create Digital Humans

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Simulations">
    In the sidebar, click **Simulations**. Click **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the callers">
    Select **Generate from Natural Language**. Describe the callers and goals you want to test. Bluejay generates the Digital Humans.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To write each Digital Human by hand, open **Digital Humans** and click **Create Custom**. See [Digital Humans](/key-concepts/digital-humans/overview).

## Run the simulation

Click **Run N Calls** in the **Create Simulation** dialog. To run the same simulation again later, open it and click **Create New Run**.

## Read the results

The run page shows pass and fail totals and one row for each Conversation. The **Outcome** column shows the result for each call. Open a row to see the **Transcript**, the audio, and each Custom Metric result.
