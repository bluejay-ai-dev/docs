A simulation sends Digital Humans to call or message your agent, then scores each Conversation with Custom Metrics.

You need a Bluejay account at [app.getbluejay.ai](https://app.getbluejay.ai). If you do not have one, [book a demo](https://cal.com/rohanv/15min).

## Connect your agent

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Connect Your Agent dialog">
    In the sidebar header, click **+** (Add agent). Enter a **Name**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the mode">
    Set **Mode** to **Voice**, **Text**, or **Email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the connection type">
    Select a tile under **Voice Connection Type** or **Text Connection Type**. Fill in the fields for that tile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the agent">
    If the tile shows **Save & Sync**, click it and select your agent from the list. Click **Create**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Find your connection type in this table. Each integration page lists the fields.

| Category | How Bluejay reaches the agent | Integrations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voice and chat platforms | Bluejay connects with your platform credentials and syncs your agents, or joins a Digital Human to your agent's room. | Your platform page under **Integrations**, for example [Vapi](/integrations/vapi) |
| Phone and SIP | Bluejay dials your phone number or SIP URI. | [Phone and SIP](/simulation-integrations/sip) |
| Custom voice endpoint | Bluejay streams audio to your WebSocket URL. | [WebSocket](/simulation-integrations/websockets) |
| Chat over HTTP | Bluejay posts Digital Human messages to your webhook URL. | [HTTP Webhook](/simulation-integrations/http-webhooks) |

For SMS, Email, and OpenAI-compatible, see [Modes and connections](/key-concepts/agents/overview#modes-and-connections).

## Create Digital Humans

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Simulations">
    In the sidebar, click **Simulations**. Click **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the callers">
    Select **Generate from Natural Language**. Describe the callers and goals you want to test. Bluejay generates the Digital Humans.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To write each Digital Human by hand, open **Digital Humans** and click **Create Custom**. See [Digital Humans](/key-concepts/digital-humans/overview).

## Run the simulation

In the **Create Simulation** dialog, review the Digital Humans and start the run.

## Read the results

The run page shows one row for each Conversation with its result. Open a row to see the **Transcript**, the audio, and each Custom Metric result.
