A simulation sends Digital Humans to call or message your agent, then scores each conversation with Custom Metrics.

You need a Bluejay account at [app.getbluejay.ai](https://app.getbluejay.ai). If you do not have one, [book a demo](https://cal.com/rohanv/15min).

## Connect your agent

Add the agent with **+** in the sidebar header. Pick the mode and connection type that match how your customers reach the agent. [Modes and connections](/key-concepts/agents/overview#modes-and-connections) lists the types, and each integration page lists its fields. If the connection shows **Save & Sync**, Bluejay imports your agents from the platform.

## Run a simulation

Create a simulation with the **Generate from Natural Language** type. Describe the callers and goals you want to test, and Bluejay generates the Digital Humans. Review them before you start the run, because each one places a conversation.

The run page shows each conversation with its result and Custom Metric results. Open the failed conversations first.
