Set `redact_pii` to `true` on a monitoring agent to redact personally identifiable information on production conversations. The default is `false`.

Set it on [`POST /v1/add-agent`](/api-reference/endpoint/add-agent), [`update-agent`](/api-reference/endpoint/update-agent), or [`update-agent-by-external-id`](/api-reference/endpoint/update-agent-by-external-id). The same switch is in the agent's observability settings.

It applies to conversations you send to [`POST /v1/evaluate`](/api-reference/endpoint/evaluate). It does not apply to simulations.

Redaction covers the transcript and spoken PII in the recording. Spoken PII is beeped. Tool call parameters, tool call outputs, events, and metadata are not redacted.

## Redacted

- Names
- Phone numbers
- Email addresses
- Locations
- Dates of birth
- Age
- Card numbers, expiration dates, and CVVs
- Account numbers and banking information
- Social Security numbers
- Driver's license numbers
- Passport numbers
- Healthcare numbers
- Usernames and passwords
- IP addresses
- Organization names
- Occupation
- Nationality
- Gender and sexuality
- Injuries
- Language
- Number sequences
- Vehicle identifiers

## Not redacted

- Blood type
- Generic dates
- Drugs
- Medical conditions
- Medical procedures
- Dollar amounts
- Political affiliation
- Religion
- URLs
- Named events
