## Get conversations into Logs

| Source | How conversations arrive |
|--------|--------------------------|
| Voice platform | After you connect the platform once, it sends each ended call to Bluejay. See the **Monitor** section of your platform page under **Integrations**, for example [Vapi](/integrations/vapi#monitor). |
| [`POST /v1/evaluate`](/api-reference/endpoint/evaluate) | Your backend sends each conversation after it ends. |
| **Upload** | Upload one **Audio Call** or **Chat/Transcript** from the Logs page. |

Bluejay runs your Custom Metrics on each conversation when it arrives.

## Find a conversation

Filter conversations by time, agent, status, issue, metric, or metadata, and save a filter as a view. You can also type a plain-text query, for example "failed calls yesterday".

Click a row to open **Conversation Details**. It shows the summary, metadata, metric results, the transcript with audio and tool calls, and the linked [Traces](/key-concepts/traces/overview).

## Act on conversations

Select one or more rows, then select an action:

- **Reevaluate**: Run the metrics again, optionally with a different Agent Version.
- **Replay**: Test the conversations against a **Target Agent** in a [simulation](/test/simulations/overview).
- **Metrics Lab**: Add the conversations to a [Metrics Lab](/key-concepts/metrics-lab/overview).
- **Delete** or **Export**.

If you disagree with a metric result, click **Edit metrics** in the conversation to correct it.
