Bluejay runs a test conversation with your agent on a schedule and notifies you when it stops working. It works for voice, SMS, and email agents.

<video controls muted playsInline preload="metadata" className="w-full rounded-xl" src="/videos/uptime-creation-demo.mp4"></video>

## Create a monitor

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the agent and schedule">
    On the **Uptime** page, click **Create** and pick the agent to check, how often, and on which days and hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the caller">
    Under **Custom Caller**, pick a Digital Human that talks like your real customers, so the test conversation follows a path they take.

    The default caller, **Health Checker**, acts as a customer asking one short question that your agent handles, written from the agent's description. It ends the conversation after the first real answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who hears about it">
    Turn on the channels to notify, then click **Create**. [Connect a channel](/monitor/alerts#connect-a-channel) first if it is not set up yet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checks

Checks decide whether a run passes. Give each monitor at least one check that grades something, or Bluejay rejects it.

| Check | Passes when |
|-------|-------------|
| **Agent Responds** | The call connected and the agent kept answering the caller. On a voice call, a silence of more than 15 seconds after the caller speaks fails the check. |
| **Agent Malfunction** | The agent did not state an error during the conversation. |
| **Latency** | The average response time stayed under the threshold. |
| **Custom metric** | One of your custom metrics met its condition. |

Email monitors also include these checks:

| Check | Passes when |
|-------|-------------|
| **Authentication** | The reply passed SPF, DKIM, and DMARC. |
| **Spam** | The reply was not marked as spam. |
| **Bounced** | The probe email was delivered. |

<Warning>
  For email monitors, allowlist `mail.bluejaysims.com` in the agent's inbox. If you do not, Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 can quarantine the probe email, and the agent never receives it.
</Warning>

## Verdicts

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Up** | All measurable checks passed. |
| **Down** | At least one check failed. |

Bluejay sends a notification only when the verdict changes between **Up** and **Down**.

## Latency

Latency is the average time from the end of the caller's turn to the start of the agent's reply. The greeting is not included.

| Channel | Default <Code>latency_threshold_ms</Code> |
|---------|--------------------------------|
| Voice | 3000 |
| SMS and webchat | 60000 |
| Email | No threshold. Bluejay reports the time from send to reply. |

## Alert after repeated failures

To ignore one-off failures, set how many failures in a row the **Latency** or **Agent Malfunction** check needs before it counts toward **Down**. You can set 1 to 50.

Keep **Agent Responds** at 1, so you hear at once when your agent stops answering.

In the [API](/api-reference/endpoint/create-uptime-monitor), this is `alert_after_failures` in the check's `config`.

## Timeouts

If a run does not finish within its timeout, Bluejay checks the agent again through a backup path when one exists. A timeout alone never makes the monitor **Down**.

| Monitor | Timeout |
|---------|---------|
| Voice | 5 minutes |
| Voice with a custom Digital Human | 10 minutes |
| SMS | 15 minutes |
| Email | 15 minutes |

## Outbound agents

Bluejay does not call an outbound agent, so add an [outbound trigger webhook](/test/simulations/reference#outbound-trigger-webhook) that starts the call, or Bluejay rejects the monitor. If your voice platform places the call for Bluejay, you can skip the webhook; its integration page says so.

For an outbound email agent, you only need its inbox address, because it sends the first email.
