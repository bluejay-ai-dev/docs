Use an alert with a [metric](/monitor/metrics) to catch failures on your production conversations.

To create alerts from code, use the [Alerts API](/api-reference/endpoint/create-alert).

## Create an alert

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    In the sidebar under **Observability**, open **Alerts** and click **Create Alert**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the trigger and scope">
    Choose what counts as a failure, and how many failures in what window raise the alert. For a metric where one failure is too many, such as hallucination, set **for at least** to 1.

    An alert watches only its own [workspace](/administration/workspaces), even when set to all agents, so create it in the workspace of the agents it should watch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who gets notified">
    Send failures that need action now to PagerDuty or Better Stack, and quality drops to Slack or email. Only [connected channels](#connect-a-channel) can be turned on.

    <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/integrations-slack-alarm-dialog.png" alt="Create Alert dialog with Name, Trigger, Scope, and Notify sections, Slack Notifications on, and a Slack preview with Send test notification" />
    <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/integrations-slack-alarm-dialog-dark.png" alt="Create Alert dialog with Name, Trigger, Scope, and Notify sections, Slack Notifications on, and a Slack preview with Send test notification" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test and create">
    Click **Send test notification**, then **Create Alert**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How alerts evaluate

An alert counts the production conversations that match its condition in the window. It enters alarm when at least N conversations match, where N is the **for at least** number, and recovers when the count drops below 80% of N (minimum 1).

With a window such as the last 50 conversations, the alert does not fire until that many conversations exist.

## Incidents

PagerDuty and Better Stack receive incidents, and your escalation policy in that tool decides who gets the page.

| Source | Opens an incident | Resolves the incident |
|---|---|---|
| Uptime monitor | When the monitor goes **Down** | When the monitor recovers |
| Alert | When the alert triggers, in PagerDuty and Better Stack | When the alert recovers, in PagerDuty and Better Stack |
