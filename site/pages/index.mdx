## Core concepts

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card>
    ### [Agent](/test/simulations/create#connect-your-agent)

    Your AI agent that talks to customers by voice, text, or email. [Connect it](/integrations/overview) to test and monitor it in Bluejay.
  </Card>
  <Card>
    ### [Digital Human](/test/simulations/digital-humans)

    A simulated customer that calls, texts, or emails your agent. Each one is a test case, and its [personas](/test/simulations/digital-humans#personas) say who calls.
  </Card>
  <Card>
    ### [Simulation](/test/simulations/create)

    A set of Digital Humans that test one agent. You can run it as many times as you need.
  </Card>
  <Card>
    ### [Metric](/test/metrics)

    A rule that grades each conversation, in simulations and in production.
  </Card>
</Columns>
