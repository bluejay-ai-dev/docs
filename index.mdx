## Get started

[Sign up at app.getbluejay.ai](https://app.getbluejay.ai/auth/sign-up), then run your first [simulation](/test/simulations/create) in three steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your agent">
    Add the voice, text, or email agent you want to test. See [Connect your agent](/test/simulations/create#connect-your-agent).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run a simulation">
    Describe the customers you want, and Bluejay generates [Digital Humans](/test/simulations/digital-humans) that call or message your agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the results">
    Open the failed conversations first. Each one shows which metric failed and where.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For the full walkthrough, see [Create a Simulation](/test/simulations/create).

## Core concepts

<div className="bj-concepts">
<Columns cols={2}>
  <div>
    ### [Agent](/test/simulations/create#connect-your-agent)

    The voice, text, or email agent you [connect](/integrations/phone-number), test, and monitor. Bluejay reads its system prompt to write Digital Humans and metrics.
  </div>
  <div>
    ### [Digital Human](/test/simulations/digital-humans)

    A simulated customer that calls, texts, or emails your agent.
  </div>
  <div>
    ### [Simulation](/test/simulations/create)

    A set of Digital Humans that test one agent. You can run it as many times as you need.
  </div>
  <div>
    ### [Metric](/test/metrics)

    A rule that grades each conversation, in simulations and in production.
  </div>
</Columns>
</div>
