## Core concepts

<div className="bj-concepts">
<Columns cols={2}>
  <div>
    ### [Agent](/test/simulations/create#connect-your-agent)

    The voice, text, or email agent you [connect](/integrations/phone-number), test, and monitor. Bluejay reads its system prompt to write Digital Humans and metrics.
  </div>
  <div>
    ### [Digital Human](/test/simulations/digital-humans)

    A simulated customer that calls, texts, or emails your agent.
  </div>
  <div>
    ### [Simulation](/test/simulations/create)

    A set of Digital Humans that test one agent. You can run it as many times as you need.
  </div>
  <div>
    ### [Metric](/test/metrics)

    A rule that grades each conversation, in simulations and in production.
  </div>
</Columns>
