## Get started

[Sign up at app.getbluejay.ai](https://app.getbluejay.ai/auth/sign-up), then run your first [simulation](/test/simulations/create) in three steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your agent">
    Add the voice, chat, or email agent you want to test. See [Connect your agent](/test/simulations/create#connect-your-agent).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run a simulation">
    Describe the customers you want, and Bluejay generates [Digital Humans](/test/simulations/digital-humans) that call or message your agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the results">
    Open the failed conversations first. Each one shows which metric failed and where.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For the full walkthrough, follow [Run Your First Simulation](/cookbook/first-simulation).

## Core concepts

<Columns cols={2}>
  <div>
    ### [Agents](/test/simulations/create#connect-your-agent)

    The agent you test and monitor. Connect it through one of the [integrations](/integrations/overview), and Bluejay reads its system prompt to write Digital Humans and custom metrics for you.
  </div>
  <div>
    ### [Knowledge Bases](/test/simulations/create#knowledge-bases)

    The facts your agent must get right. Bluejay flags answers that contradict them and writes Digital Humans that ask about every topic.
  </div>
</Columns>
