The 1-minute guide to easily create your agent/workflow test cases, with three core principles.

## 1. Write simple intent and success criteria.

Aim for simple, concise intent and success criteria fields.

Digital Humans are agentic systems. They follow instructions best with shorter prompts. Writing shorter prompts will also make it much easier for you (a human) to read over your own tests!

To minimize your intent length, configure the smaller details with the configuration options of Digital Humans (see next principle).

To minimize your success criteria length, configure separate evaluations with [custom metrics](/key-concepts/custom-metrics/overview).

<Tip>
If you reach for a word like "correctly," "appropriately," or "successfully" in an intent, you are usually describing what the agent should do. Move that idea to the success criteria and rewrite the intent in terms of the Digital Human's actions.
</Tip>

## 2. Maximize determinism.

Deterministic behavior is when you can clearly define an "if this, then that". Any deterministic behavior should try to be moved to a [configuration](/key-concepts/digital-humans/configuration) option in the Digital Human.

Examples:

- For case-specific information like ID or DoB, use Digital Human **traits**.
- For "if agent says this, human does that", use **scripted responses**.
- For human or speech-specific behavior like language, hangup behavior, and more, use **behaviors**.

## 3. Define good agent context.

One of Bluejay's many strengths is to create test cases you can't see. To maximize this strength, provide as much agent context as possible.

Four ways to provide context:

1. Ensure the agent description describes what your agent (or system) does in the **Agent Settings** tab.
2. If your agent has a [workflow](/key-concepts/agents/workflow), ensure your agent's workflow is properly synced or is described accurately in the agent description.
3. Upload any documents related to your system or agent by creating a knowledge base for the agent in the **Knowledge Bases** tab.
4. Verify your agent context with Jay (Bluejay AI) in the platform. Ask it about your agent and what it thinks about its context.
