The Digital Human acts as the voicemail machine. It plays a greeting and the beep, then records your agent's message.

Bluejay writes success criteria that check the message is clear and fits the reason for the call.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the type">
    [Create a simulation](/test/simulations/create#create-a-simulation) and click the **Voicemail** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the greeting">
    Under **Greeting mode**, choose one of these options:

    - **Custom Message**: write the greeting. Bluejay replaces `[name]` with the name of each Digital Human.
    - **Standard greeting**: Bluejay plays a standard greeting with the name and the language of the Digital Human.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the number of calls">
    Set **Number of calls** from 1 to 100.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Click **Save**, then click **Generate Digital Humans**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Limits

- Outbound agents only. For an inbound agent, the card is disabled.
