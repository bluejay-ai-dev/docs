This type checks the message your agent leaves when nobody picks up. The Digital Human acts like a voicemail box: it plays a greeting, beeps, and records what your agent says.

<video controls muted playsInline preload="metadata" className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" src="/videos/sim-type-voicemail.mp4"></video>

## When to use it

- Use this when your agent makes outbound calls, like reminders or follow-ups, and often reaches voicemail.
- Use this to check that the message says who is calling, why, and what the person should do next.
- Use this to check that your agent waits for the beep instead of talking over the greeting.

You only see this type when the selected agent is an outbound agent.

## How it works

1. You pick a greeting mode. **Standard greeting** plays a normal voicemail greeting with the Digital Human's name. With **Custom Message**, write your own and put `[name]` where the name goes and Bluejay fills it in.
2. You set **Number of calls**. Each call is one Digital Human.
3. You click **Continue**, check the **Review** page, and run.

Bluejay writes the success criteria for you: the message must say who is calling, why, and what happens next.

## Limits

- Outbound agents only.
- One voicemail test makes a maximum of 100 Digital Humans.
