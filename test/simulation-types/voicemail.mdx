Use this type to check the message your agent leaves when a customer does not pick up.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the card">
    [Create a simulation](/test/simulations/create#create-a-simulation) and select the **Voicemail** card.

    <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/create-simulation-card-voicemail.png" alt="Voicemail card in the Create Simulation type picker" />
    <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/create-simulation-card-voicemail-dark.png" alt="Voicemail card in the Create Simulation type picker" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the greeting">
    The Digital Human plays this greeting and the beep, then records your agent's message. Bluejay writes success criteria that check your agent says who it is, why it called, and what happens next.

    - With **Custom Message**, write `[name]` where the Digital Human's name goes. Bluejay fills in each Digital Human's name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Limits

- Outbound agents only.
- One voicemail test makes a maximum of 100 Digital Humans.
