The Digital Human acts as the voicemail machine. It plays a greeting and the beep, then records your agent's message.

Bluejay writes success criteria that check the message is clear and fits the reason for the call.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the card">
    [Create a simulation](/test/simulations/create#create-a-simulation) and select the **Voicemail** card.

    <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/create-simulation-card-voicemail.png" alt="Voicemail card in the Create Simulation type picker" />
    <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/create-simulation-card-voicemail-dark.png" alt="Voicemail card in the Create Simulation type picker" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the greeting">
    Choose a **Greeting mode**:

    - **Custom Message**: you write the greeting. Bluejay replaces `[name]` with the name of each Digital Human.
    - **Standard greeting**: Bluejay plays a standard greeting with the name and the language of the Digital Human.

    Then click **Save** and **Generate Digital Humans**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Limits

- Outbound agents only.
