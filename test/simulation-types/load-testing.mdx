Use this type before a launch or a busy period, to see how your agent and your telephony hold up when many customers contact it at once.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the type">
    [Create a simulation](/test/simulations/create#create-a-simulation) and click the **Load Testing** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the number of calls">
    Set **Number of calls** from 1 to 100. Each call is one Digital Human.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the concurrency">
    On the **Simulation Settings** tab, set **Max concurrent conversations** to the load you want to test.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Click **Save**, then click **Generate Digital Humans**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Limits

- One load test makes a maximum of 100 Digital Humans.
- For an Outbound agent that uses the Bluejay phone pool, the **Max concurrent conversations** setting does not show.
- Your provider can have its own concurrent-call limit. If a call goes over it, the call ends with [`CUSTOMER_CONCURRENCY_LIMIT`](/test/simulations/create#error-codes).
