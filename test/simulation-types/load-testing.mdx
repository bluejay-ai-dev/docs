Use this type before a launch or a busy period, to see how your agent and your telephony hold up when many customers contact it at once.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the type">
    [Create a simulation](/test/simulations/create#create-a-simulation) and click the **Load Testing** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the load and generate">
    Each call in **Number of calls** is one Digital Human. On the **Simulation Settings** tab, set **Max concurrent conversations** to the load you want to test. Then click **Save** and **Generate Digital Humans**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Limits

- One load test makes a maximum of 100 Digital Humans.
- For an outbound agent that uses the Bluejay phone pool, the **Max concurrent conversations** setting does not show.
- Your provider can have its own concurrent-call limit. If a call goes over it, the call ends with [`CUSTOMER_CONCURRENCY_LIMIT`](/test/simulations/reference#error-codes).

## Larger load tests

To test more than 100 calls, [contact the Bluejay team](https://cal.com/rohanv/15min).
