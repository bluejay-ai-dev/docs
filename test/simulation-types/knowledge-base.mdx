Use this type to check that your agent answers correctly across everything it must know. Bluejay groups the documents of a [Knowledge Base](/test/simulations/create#knowledge-bases) into topics, then makes Digital Humans that ask about each topic.

## Before you start

Connect the Knowledge Base to the agent, and wait until its documents show **Ready**.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the type">
    [Create a simulation](/test/simulations/create#create-a-simulation) and click the **Generate from Knowledge Base** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Knowledge Base">
    Choose it under **Knowledge base**. The list shows only the Knowledge Bases connected to the agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the number of Digital Humans">
    Set **Digital humans** from 1 to 100. The default is 10.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Click **Save**, then click **Generate Digital Humans**. Each Digital Human shows the topic that it tests.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To score the answers against the Knowledge Base, turn on **Enable Hallucination Evaluations** for your organization, or use a [custom metric](/test/custom-metrics/overview) that searches the Knowledge Base.
