A Knowledge Base is the set of documents your agent answers from, like your FAQ, prices, or opening hours. This type writes questions from those documents and checks that your agent's answers match them.

<video controls muted playsInline preload="metadata" className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" src="/videos/sim-type-knowledge-base.mp4"></video>

## When to use it

- Use this when your agent answers questions from documents, and a wrong answer would hurt, like a wrong price or wrong hours.
- Use this after you update the documents, to check that the agent picked up the change.
- Use this when you want broad coverage without writing tests yourself.

## How it works

1. You connect the [Knowledge Base](/test/simulations/reference#knowledge-bases) to the agent and wait until its documents show **Ready**.
2. You pick the type. Bluejay groups the documents into topics and writes tests spread across them, each with a success criteria. You can make 1 to 100 Digital Humans.
3. You add [personas](/test/simulations/personas), or click **Skip personas**.
4. On the **Review** page you check the Digital Humans, then run them.

If the agent has no Knowledge Base yet, the step offers **Add a knowledge base** instead.

## Score the answers

To score the answers against the Knowledge Base, turn on **Enable Hallucination Evaluations** for your organization, or use a [custom metric](/test/metrics) that searches the Knowledge Base.
