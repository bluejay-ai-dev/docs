Use this type to check that your agent answers correctly across everything it must know.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the Knowledge Base">
    Connect the [Knowledge Base](/test/simulations/reference#knowledge-bases) to the agent, and wait until its documents show **Ready**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the card">
    [Create a simulation](/test/simulations/create#create-a-simulation) and select the **Generate from Knowledge Base** card. Bluejay groups the documents into topics and spreads the Digital Humans across them, so there is nothing to write. You can make 1 to 100 Digital Humans.

    <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/create-simulation-card-natural-language-knowledge-base.png" alt="Generate from Knowledge Base card in the Create Simulation type picker" />
    <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/create-simulation-card-natural-language-knowledge-base-dark.png" alt="Generate from Knowledge Base card in the Create Simulation type picker" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Digital Humans">
    Edit any Digital Human that does not test what you meant.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Score the answers

To score the answers against the Knowledge Base, turn on **Enable Hallucination Evaluations** for your organization, or use a [custom metric](/test/metrics) that searches the Knowledge Base.
