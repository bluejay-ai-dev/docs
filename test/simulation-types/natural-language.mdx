## Describe the callers

Click the **Generate from Natural Language** card when you know what you want to test. For example, write "callers who ask for a refund after the 30-day window" in the text box, and Bluejay makes callers that do that. You can make 1 to 100 Digital Humans.

![The Generate from Natural Language settings with a prompt describing dental-cleaning callers, the Quick Templates above it, and the number of calls set to 1](/images/create-simulation-natural-language.png)

## Goal Adherence

Click the **Goal Adherence** card to check that your agent does its main jobs, such as "Complete booking" or "Handle refund".

Click **Auto-generate** to read the goals from the agent's system prompt, then select the goals to test and how many Digital Humans each one gets.

Bluejay saves the goals on the agent, so the next simulation for that agent starts with the same list. To skip a goal in one run without deleting it, clear its checkbox.

## Knowledge Base

Click the **Generate from Knowledge Base** card to check that your agent answers correctly across everything it must know. Bluejay groups the documents of a [Knowledge Base](/test/simulations/reference#knowledge-bases) into topics, then makes Digital Humans that ask about each one.

First connect the Knowledge Base to the agent and wait until its documents show **Ready**. **Digital humans** goes from 1 to 100 and starts at 10.

To score the answers against the Knowledge Base, turn on **Enable Hallucination Evaluations** for your organization, or use a [custom metric](/test/metrics) that searches the Knowledge Base.

## System Prompt

Click the **Generate from System Prompt** card to check that your agent follows its own instructions. Bluejay finds the rules in the prompt and makes two Digital Humans for each rule: one that the agent must help, and one that it must push back on.

**Digital humans** starts at two per rule, which tests every rule. You can set a lower number, from 1 to 200, but then some rules go untested. A prompt with more than 100 rules is never fully tested, and a prompt with no rules Bluejay can test cannot use this type.
