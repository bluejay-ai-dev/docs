These types turn text into Digital Humans, so you do not write each one yourself. They differ only in where the text comes from, and they all work the same way:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the type">
    [Create a simulation](/test/simulations/create#create-a-simulation) and click the card named in the section below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give Bluejay the text">
    Write a description, list goals, or let Bluejay read the system prompt or a Knowledge Base.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Set how many Digital Humans to make, click **Save**, then click **Generate Digital Humans**. Read the list before you run it, and edit any Digital Human that does not test what you meant.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Describe the callers

Click the **Generate from Natural Language** card when you know what you want to test. For example, write "callers who ask for a refund after the 30-day window" in the text box, and Bluejay makes callers that do that. You can make 1 to 100 Digital Humans.

To start from a preset prompt, click one of the **Quick Templates**:

- **Edge Cases**
- **Error Handling**
- **Stress Testing**

## Goal Adherence

Click the **Goal Adherence** card to check that your agent does its main jobs, such as "Complete booking" or "Handle refund". The dialog selects this card first.

Type a goal in **Add a goal** and click **Add**, or click **Auto-generate** to read the goals from the agent's system prompt. Then, in the **Goals** list, select the goals to test and set how many Digital Humans each one gets.

Bluejay saves the goals on the agent, so the next simulation for that agent starts with the same list. To skip a goal in one run without deleting it, clear its checkbox.

## System Prompt

Click the **Generate from System Prompt** card to check that your agent follows its own instructions. Bluejay shows the system prompt next to the rules it found, and makes two Digital Humans for each rule: one that the agent must help, and one that it must push back on.

**Digital humans** starts at two per rule, which tests every rule. You can set a lower number, from 1 to 200, but then Bluejay shows how many rules go untested. A prompt with more than 100 rules is never fully tested, and a prompt with no rules Bluejay can test cannot use this type.

## Knowledge Base

Click the **Generate from Knowledge Base** card to check that your agent answers correctly across everything it must know. Bluejay groups the documents of a [Knowledge Base](/test/simulations/create#knowledge-bases) into topics, then makes Digital Humans that ask about each one.

First connect the Knowledge Base to the agent and wait until its documents show **Ready**, because the **Knowledge base** list shows only connected Knowledge Bases. **Digital humans** goes from 1 to 100 and starts at 10. Each Digital Human shows the topic it tests.

To score the answers against the Knowledge Base, turn on **Enable Hallucination Evaluations** for your organization, or use a [custom metric](/test/custom-metrics/overview) that searches the Knowledge Base.
