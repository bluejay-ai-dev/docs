## Natural Language

Use **Generate from Natural Language** when you know what you want to test. Describe the callers, for example "callers who ask for a refund after the 30-day window". You can make 1 to 100 Digital Humans.

## Goal Adherence

Use **Goal Adherence** to check that your agent does its main jobs, such as "Complete booking" or "Handle refund". To start from the agent's system prompt, click **Auto-generate**.

- Bluejay saves the goals on the agent, so the next simulation for that agent starts with the same list.
- To skip a goal in one run without deleting it, clear its checkbox.

## Knowledge Base

Use **Generate from Knowledge Base** to check that your agent answers correctly across everything it must know. Bluejay groups the documents of the [Knowledge Base](/test/simulations/reference#knowledge-bases) into topics and spreads the Digital Humans across them.

- Connect the Knowledge Base to the agent first, and wait until its documents show **Ready**.
- You can make 1 to 100 Digital Humans. The default is 10.
- To score the answers against the Knowledge Base, turn on **Enable Hallucination Evaluations** for your organization, or use a [custom metric](/test/metrics) that searches the Knowledge Base.

## System Prompt

Use **Generate from System Prompt** to check that your agent follows its own instructions. Bluejay finds the rules in the prompt and tests each rule from both sides: one Digital Human that the agent must help, and one that it must push back on.

- **Digital humans** starts at two per rule, which tests every rule. A lower number leaves the last rules of the prompt untested.
- The maximum is 200, so a prompt with more than 100 rules is never fully tested.
- A prompt with no rules that Bluejay can test cannot use this type.
