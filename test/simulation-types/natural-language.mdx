Here you just describe what you want to test, in your own words, in the **What do you want to test?** box. [Bluejay AI](/improve/bluejay-ai) reads your agent, plans the tests, and writes the Digital Humans for you.

<video controls muted playsInline preload="metadata" className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" src="/videos/sim-type-natural-language.mp4"></video>

## When to use it

- Use this when you know what you want to test but do not want to pick a type or fill in fields.
- Use this for a mix of things at once, like "booking, cancelling, and one angry caller".
- Use this when you have a file of test cases or call transcripts. Attach it to the box and Bluejay builds the tests from it.

## How it works

1. You pick your agent, then write what to test, for example "callers who want to book, move or cancel a cleaning, plus one caller with tooth pain".
2. Bluejay AI reads the agent's prompt and your words, explains what it plans to test, and writes the Digital Humans. **Depth** sets how many it makes. **Auto** lets Bluejay decide.
3. Check the Digital Humans. Ask Bluejay AI for changes in the chat, or edit a Digital Human yourself.
4. Click **Run simulation** when it looks right.
