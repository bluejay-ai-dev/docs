---

Use this type when you know what you want to test but do not want to write each Digital Human yourself. For example, write "callers who ask for a refund after the 30-day window" and Bluejay makes callers that do that.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the type">
    [Create a simulation](/test/simulations/create#create-a-simulation) and click the **Generate from Natural Language** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the callers">
    Write what the Digital Humans must do in the text box. To start from a preset prompt, click one of the **Quick Templates**:

    - **Edge Cases**
    - **Error Handling**
    - **Stress Testing**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the number of calls">
    Set how many Digital Humans to make, from 1 to 100.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Click **Save**, then click **Generate Digital Humans**. Read the list before you run it, and edit a Digital Human if it does not test what you meant.
  </Step>
</Steps>
