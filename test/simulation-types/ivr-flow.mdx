An IVR is a phone menu, the "press 1 for billing, press 2 for appointments" recording you hear when you call a business. In this type the Digital Human plays that menu, and Bluejay checks that your agent picks the right options to get where it needs to go.

<video controls muted playsInline preload="metadata" className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" src="/videos/sim-type-ivr-flow.mp4"></video>

## When to use it

- Use this when your agent calls businesses that answer with a phone menu, like a pharmacy or an insurance line.
- Use this to check that your agent presses the right keys or says the right words, and waits on hold when it has to.
- Use this when a change to the menu on the other side might break your agent's calls.

## How it works

1. You draw the phone menu once in **Scenario Builder** (see [Build the flow](#build-the-flow)).
2. In the simulation you tick the IVR Flows to run. Each one becomes one Digital Human that goes through the whole menu in one call.
3. You click **Continue**, check the **Review** page, and run.

## Build the flow

In **Scenario Builder**, draw the phone menu and what your agent does at each step. The Digital Human plays this menu, and your agent calls through it.

An IVR Flow shows up in the simulation only once it has at least one IVR Menu. For a full example, see [Test an Agent That Calls Through a Phone Menu](/cookbook/ivr-phone-menu).

## Limits

- Meant for outbound voice agents, since your agent is the one calling through the menu.
