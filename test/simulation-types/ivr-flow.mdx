When your agent calls a business, it often meets a phone menu (IVR) before it reaches a person. In this type, the Digital Human plays that phone system and your agent is the caller. You draw the menu on the **Scenario Builder** page, and Bluejay checks that your agent presses the right keys and stays on the line through hold.

## Build the IVR Flow

On the **Scenario Builder** page, click **Create** and choose **Create IVR Flow**. Then add nodes from the toolbox.

The **IVR / Digital Human** section has the nodes for the phone system:

- **IVR Menu**: a menu that the caller hears. Add one option for each choice, for example "press 2 for billing".
- **Hold**: hold music, with a **Hold duration**.
- **Human Agent**: a live representative that Bluejay plays after the menu, with its own intent and success criteria.

The **Your Agent** section has what your agent does:

- **Message**: what your agent says.
- **DTMF**: the key that your agent presses.

Connect each menu option to the step that comes next, then click **Save**. For a full example, see [Test an Agent That Calls Through a Phone Menu](/cookbook/ivr-phone-menu).

## Run it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the type">
    Click **Test** in the Scenario Builder toolbar, which opens a new simulation with the **IVR Flow** type selected. Or [create a simulation](/test/simulations/create#create-a-simulation) and click the **IVR Flow** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the IVR Flows">
    Select one or more IVR Flows in the list. To build a new one from here, click **New IVR Flow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Click **Save**, then click **Generate Digital Humans**. Each IVR Flow becomes one Digital Human that goes through the whole phone tree in one call.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Limits

- Outbound voice agents only. For an Inbound agent, or for a chat, SMS, or email agent, the card is disabled.
