When your agent calls a business, it often meets a phone menu (IVR) before it reaches a person. In this type, the Digital Human plays that phone system and your agent is the caller. You draw the menu in Scenario Builder, and Bluejay checks that your agent presses the right keys and stays on the line through hold.

## Build the flow

On the **Scenario Builder** page, create an IVR flow and draw the phone system from the toolbox. Give each menu one option per choice, for example "press 2 for billing". You can add hold music, and a human agent at the end with its own intent and success criteria.

Between those steps, add what your agent does: a message or a key press. Then connect each menu option to the step that comes next. For a full example, see [Test an Agent That Calls Through a Phone Menu](/cookbook/ivr-phone-menu).

## Run it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the type">
    Click **Test** in the Scenario Builder toolbar to open a new simulation with this type selected, or [create a simulation](/test/simulations/create#create-a-simulation) and pick the IVR Flow card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the IVR Flows">
    Select one or more IVR Flows in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Click **Save**, then click **Generate Digital Humans**. Each IVR Flow becomes one Digital Human that goes through the whole phone tree in one call.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Limits

- Outbound voice agents only. For an inbound agent, or for a chat, SMS, or email agent, the card is disabled.
