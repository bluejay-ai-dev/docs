## Build a journey

A journey is an ordered list of steps. Each step is one call with its own intent, for example "Book appointment" and then "Reschedule". The Digital Human keeps the same identity across the steps, so later calls test how your agent treats a returning customer.

![Customer Journeys card on the Create Simulation dialog](/images/journey-card.png)

![Customer Journeys configuration: steps on the left, preview on the right](/images/journey-config-panel.png)

Total calls = journeys × steps. For example, 10 journeys with 3 steps place 30 calls.

If a call does not connect or drops, Bluejay skips the next steps of that journey with the status `STEP_SKIPPED`.

## Results

The calls of one journey group into one row on the run page.
