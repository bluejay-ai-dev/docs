A persona is a reusable caller: their personality, voice and call conditions. Keep a set of personas on the **Personas** tab and make Digital Humans from them with any of your [test cases](/test/simulations/test-cases).

<video controls muted playsInline preload="metadata" className="w-full rounded-xl" src="/videos/personas-tab.mp4"></video>

## Make Digital Humans

Select personas on the **Personas** tab, open **Actions**, choose **Create digital humans**, and pick the test cases to pair them with. Bluejay makes one Digital Human for each test case and persona.

- Each Digital Human copies the persona when it is made and is tagged `Persona: <name>`.
- After that it is an ordinary Digital Human. Its persona can't be swapped; make another Digital Human from the other persona instead.
- An edit to a persona changes only Digital Humans you make afterwards.

## Fields

| Field | Write or set |
|---|---|
| Name | Who this caller is, for example "Rushed commuter". Names are unique in a workspace. |
| Personality Description | Who the caller is and their attitude, in one short line, for example "retiree on a family plan, hard of hearing". |
| Voice | Language, accent and gender, or a custom voice. |

There is no emotion setting. To make the caller angry, rushed or confused, write that attitude into **Personality Description**. The behavior fields are the same as on a [Digital Human](/test/simulations/digital-humans#behavior).

## The Default persona

Each workspace has a **Default persona**, a plain caller with an American voice and no background noise. A plan uses it when you do not pick a persona. You can edit it, but not delete it.

## Write a good persona

- Describe the person, never the task. "Cancel my plan" belongs in the test case.
- Make each persona differ from the others in one way that matters to your agent, such as a strong accent, a noisy line, or an impatient caller.
- Keep personas few and reuse them. Each persona doubles the Digital Humans you make from a set of test cases.

## Personas and Personalities

A persona is copied into a Digital Human when you make it. A [Personality](/test/simulations/digital-humans#personalities) is picked on a simulation and reruns every Digital Human in it with different caller behavior.
