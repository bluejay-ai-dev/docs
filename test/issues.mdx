With Issues, Bluejay identifies common problems with your agent from simulation results.

If you are tracking issues from production calls, see [Issues](/monitor/issues).

<img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/issues-list-dark.png" alt="The simulation Issues list with one row for each issue, showing its priority, status, failure mode, conversation count, and percent of conversations" />

## How issues work

After Bluejay evaluates a simulation conversation, it finds where the agent went wrong and matches each failure to similar failures from your other simulation conversations.

Every simulation issue is an **Agent Failure**: the agent had a failure based on its conversations (transcripts). Tool and phone provider errors become issues only in [production](/monitor/issues).

## Work through an issue

<img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/issue-detail.png" alt="An issue's detail page with its description, impacted conversation count, occurrences over time, impacted simulation conversations, and a properties panel for priority, status, and assignee" />
<img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/issue-detail-dark.png" alt="An issue's detail page with its description, impacted conversation count, occurrences over time, impacted simulation conversations, and a properties panel for priority, status, and assignee" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the top issue">
    Start with the issue that hits the most conversations, and read a few of its conversations to see what the agent did.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the cause">
    Click **Investigate** to ask [Bluejay AI](/improve/bluejay-ai) what to change, or **Fix** to copy a prompt for your coding assistant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track the work">
    Set **Priority**, **Status**, and **Assignee**. After your fix, add a [custom metric](/test/metrics) that checks for the failure, and rerun the simulation.
  </Step>
</Steps>
