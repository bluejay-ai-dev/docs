Enterprise organizations can sign in to Bluejay through their identity provider (IdP) with SAML single sign-on, and let that IdP add and remove people automatically with SCIM 2.0. Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, OneLogin, and any other SAML 2.0 and SCIM 2.0 provider work.

An organization admin sets up both under **Settings → Security → Organization**.

## Single sign-on (SAML)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a SAML app in your IdP">
    Create a SAML 2.0 application named Bluejay with these values:

    | Field | Value |
    |-------|-------|
    | Single sign-on URL (ACS URL) | `https://auth.getbluejay.ai/auth/v1/sso/saml/acs` |
    | Audience URI (SP Entity ID) | `https://auth.getbluejay.ai/auth/v1/sso/saml/metadata` |
    | Name ID format | `EmailAddress` |
    | Application username | Email |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign people">
    Assign the app to everyone who should have Bluejay access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on SSO in Bluejay">
    In **Settings → Security → Organization → Single Sign-On**, paste your IdP's metadata URL (or the metadata XML) and the email domains that should use SSO, then save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Your team opens [app.getbluejay.ai](https://app.getbluejay.ai), clicks **Continue with SSO**, and enters their work email. Bluejay sends them to your IdP and back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Once SSO is on, everyone with an email on those domains signs in through your IdP. Start sign-in from Bluejay's login page; signing in from the IdP's app tile is not supported yet.
</Note>

SSO signs people in; it does not decide who belongs to your organization. A person needs to be a member already, or have a pending invite, before their first SSO sign-in. Invite people from the **Team** page, or let your IdP do it with SCIM below.

A person's first SSO sign-in creates a new Bluejay account for them in your organization. If they also had a password or Google account, remove the old one from the **Team** page once SSO works for them.

## User provisioning (SCIM)

With SCIM, assigning someone to the Bluejay app in your IdP gives them access, and removing them takes it away. Nobody has to invite or remove people by hand.

SSO must be turned on first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Generate a token in Bluejay">
    In **Settings → Security → User provisioning (SCIM)**, click **Generate token**. Copy the base URL and the token. The token is shown once.

    | Setting | Value |
    |---------|-------|
    | SCIM base URL | `https://api.getbluejay.ai/scim/v2` |
    | Authentication | Bearer token: `Authorization: Bearer <token>`. Sending the token alone in `Authorization` also works. |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your IdP">
    On the Bluejay app in your IdP, turn on SCIM provisioning. Enter the base URL and the token, set the unique identifier to `userName` (email), and test the connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what syncs">
    Turn on **Create users**, **Update user attributes**, and **Deactivate users**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign people">
    Assign people, or groups of people, to the Bluejay app. Each one is provisioned.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### What happens

| In your IdP | In Bluejay |
|-------------|------------|
| Assign a person | They can sign in with SSO right away. No invite email is sent. |
| Change their name | Their name updates in Bluejay. |
| Unassign or deactivate them | They lose access to your organization on their next request: they are signed out and their API keys in your organization are revoked. Their access to other organizations is unaffected. |
| Assign them again | They can sign in again. Their work stays in your organization. |

### Details

- Everyone provisioned joins as a **Member**. Change roles on the **Team** page.
- Only emails on your organization's SSO domains can be provisioned.
- Groups are not synced as Bluejay roles or workspaces. Assigning a group to the app provisions each person in it.
- Clicking **Generate token** again replaces the token; the old one stops working right away, so update your IdP. **Revoke** stops syncing; people who already have access keep it.

### Supported endpoints

Bluejay implements SCIM 2.0 users: `GET`, `POST`, `PUT`, `PATCH`, and `DELETE` on `/Users`, with `userName eq` and `externalId eq` filters, plus `/ServiceProviderConfig`, `/Schemas`, and `/ResourceTypes`.
