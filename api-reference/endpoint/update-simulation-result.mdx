Adds tool calls, events, tags, metadata, and trace IDs to a simulation result.

## Identify the result

Send one of these:

- `simulation_result_id`. For SIP calls, read it from the `X-Simulation-Result-Id` header of the call.
- `start_time`, `end_time`, and `duration` in seconds.

With the time window, Bluejay looks for a connected simulation in the workspaces your key can see. A stored call matches when both times are within 90 seconds and the stored duration is within the larger of 5 seconds and 10% of `duration`. Calls with no stored duration always match.

Without `recorded_transcript`, exactly one call must match. With a transcript, Bluejay scores each call that matches:

- One call is kept only when the transcript shares at least 80% of its normalized words.
- Two or more calls are separated only when the best score leads the next score by at least 0.08.

## Errors

- 404: nothing in your organization matched the time window.
- 409: the transcript was missing, not stored yet, too close to separate the calls, or not close enough to the only call. Send `simulation_result_id` instead. Nothing is written.
