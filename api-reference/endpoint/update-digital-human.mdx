Updates a Digital Human. Validation uses the `tag` in the body. If you do not send `tag`, validation uses the stored `tag`.

`success_criteria` is one string with a bullet per line. Sending it replaces every bullet.

`persona_ids` sets the [personas](/test/simulations/digital-humans#personas) the Digital Human runs as: once as each, or once as the baseline with `[]`. Leave it out to keep them as they are. An ID that is not a persona in your organization returns `400`.

Send `voice_id` to switch to an exact voice from [GET /v1/voices](/api-reference/endpoint/list-voices). [Set a voice](/api-reference/endpoint/list-voices#set-a-voice) explains what changing `language`, `accent` or `gender` does.
