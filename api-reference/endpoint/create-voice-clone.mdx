Clones a voice from your audio clips, so a Digital Human can speak in it. Send up to 25 clips in `files`.

- The clone speaks any language. `language` only sets the language of its preview clip.
- `gender` sets grammatical gender in languages that have it.
- The clone belongs to your workspace. Only Digital Humans and personas in that workspace can use it.

The response has `voice_id`, `catalog_voice_id`, `name`, `gender` and `created_at_unix`. Pass `catalog_voice_id` as `voice_id` on a Digital Human or persona, or pass `voice_id` as `custom_voice_id`. The other voice clone endpoints take `voice_id` in the path.

Rate limit: 100 requests per minute.
