Returns the languages, accents and voices your workspace can give a Digital Human or persona. It is the live list of what you can pick. A voice ID never changes, so it is safe to store.

- `voices` has one entry per voice and language. A voice that speaks three languages appears three times.
- Your organization's cloned and generated voices come last, with `own: true` and no `accent`. They speak any language. The `accent` filter does not apply to them, and the `gender` filter keeps the ones with no gender.
- The stock voices depend on the voice providers your organization has.
- `sample_url` expires after 15 minutes. Call again for a new one.

Rate limit: 100 requests per minute.

## Set a voice

[Create Digital Humans](/api-reference/endpoint/create-digital-humans-v2), [Update Digital Human](/api-reference/endpoint/update-digital-human), [Create Persona](/api-reference/endpoint/create-persona) and [Update Persona](/api-reference/endpoint/update-persona) take a voice in one of two ways:

- **`voice_id`** pins that exact voice, such as `cartesia-connie`. It must speak the Digital Human's `language`. If you create one with `voice_id` and no `language`, Bluejay uses English, or another language the voice speaks.
- **`language`, `accent` and `gender`** pin the voice that `defaults` lists for that combination. A `language` alone uses its `default_accent`. Without `gender`, Bluejay picks one at random. A combination with no voice returns `400` with the valid choices.

A Digital Human created with no voice fields gets an English voice with a random gender. A persona has a voice only once you set `accent`, `voice_id` or `custom_voice_id`. Until then, each Digital Human that runs as it keeps its own voice.

On an update, a voice field counts only when it differs from the stored value. Changing `accent` or `gender` without `voice_id` pins that combination's default voice. Changing only `language` keeps the voice if it speaks the new language. If it does not, also send an `accent` of the new language or a `voice_id`.

- **Cloned voices:** pin one with its `voice_id` from this list, or set `custom_voice_id` to the `voice_id` from [Create Voice Clone](/api-reference/endpoint/create-voice-clone). Bluejay stores no accent or gender for a clone, so both come back null. Only Digital Humans and personas in the clone's workspace can use it.
- **Fluency:** `fluency` works only on voices with `supports_fluency`, which are ElevenLabs voices in English or Urdu. Bluejay saves `native` for any other voice.
- **Older values:** older accent values still work on input. So do the language codes `ml` for multilingual and `ad` for auto-detect, which responses return as `multi` and `auto`.
